🛡️ CareBridge AI — Clinical Safety Framework

1. Core Clinical Directive

Medical faithfulness is strictly more important than fluency. CareBridge AI is built exclusively for communicating existing clinician-provided discharge instructions in patient-friendly English and Telugu. It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, or alter clinical treatment regimens.

2. The 11-Point Medical Faithfulness Gate

Every generated care plan undergoes an automated 11-point audit before presentation:

  1. Every source medication is fully represented.
  2. Medication strengths and numeric dose quantities are strictly preserved.
  3. Measurement units (mg, mcg, ml, puffs) are preserved.
  4. Dosing frequency (OD, BD, TDS, PRN) is preserved.
  5. Route of administration (oral, sublingual, inhaler) is preserved.
  6. Treatment duration and start/stop dates are preserved.
  7. Follow-up appointment dates and times are exact.
  8. Emergency warning signs (red flags) are never omitted.
  9. Dietary, activity, and wound-care instructions match source facts.
  10. No unsupported clinical instructions or medications are invented.
  11. Telugu translations faithfully preserve all clinical parameters.

3. Hard Safety Gate Policy

If any discrepancy is detected (e.g. an altered dose from 500 mg to 5000 mg, an altered frequency from twice daily to once daily, or an omitted warning sign), the application immediately triggers the Hard Safety Gate: The plan is marked with a prominent amber/red alert: CLINICIAN REVIEW REQUIRED, with verbatim source evidence cited for manual review.

4. Readability Scoring Methodology

English readability is assessed using the established Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level formula, aiming for Class 8 English suitable for adult patients and family caregivers. For Telugu, English syllable formulas are recognized as linguistically invalid; a dedicated Dravidian script assessment reports word count, script purity, and semantic consistency instead.
